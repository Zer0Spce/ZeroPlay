# ZeroPlay v2.2.6.7 — MappleTV Ad and Watch Options Hotfix

## MappleTV-only improvements
- Hide the pill-shaped **Watch options** control after 3 seconds without actual user input. Restore it on pointer, touch, keyboard or focus activity.
- Dismiss the specific intrusive "Watch hit movies and hottest new releases in Full HD! Get access?" overlay detected on the MappleTV watch page. Does not automatically click ads.
- Use an exact `mapple.fun/watch/` host and path guard. Other video providers and their controls are unchanged.
- Preserve fullscreen, subtitles, video and play/pause surfaces.
- The existing ad-domain network filters remain active. MappleTV's ad elements may change, so protection is best effort.

## Official builds
- Signed Android and Android TV APKs, regular and Lite.
- Windows x64 ZIP with visible standalone ZeroPlayUpdater.exe.
- Web player updated to version 2.2.6.7 (cross-origin iframe content is outside the web app's direct DOM access).

Published releases require green CI for both platforms.
