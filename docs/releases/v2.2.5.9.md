# ZeroPlay v2.2.5.9 — Top 10 New Streaming Movies & Crimson Player

Release candidate date: November 13, 2026.

## What's new
- **Crimson Red embedded player theme:** VidStuck and ZXCStream now receive the ZeroPlay Crimson accent `DF2531` instead of the former turquoise `65E6CC`. The VidStuck URL continues to request `branding=ZeroPlay`. The official red Z/playmark and red/white wordmark remain the application identity.
- **One featured carousel mode:** Replaced the former six-option homepage-carousel picker (This Week Top Picks, Watchlist, Recommended Movies, Popular Movies, Now Playing, Continue Watching) with **Top 10 New Streaming Movies · US** on Windows and Android Mobile/TV/Lite.
- **Eight specific services:** Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, HBO Max, Apple TV+, Paramount+, Peacock and YouTube TV. Movies are drawn from TMDB/JustWatch watch-provider IDs matching these services **in the United States**, filtered for subscription/flat-rate availability. Services absent from the US TMDB movie-provider catalog, including YouTube TV when unlisted, are skipped rather than silently substituted.
- **Fresh rotating selections:** Current cinema-to-streaming movie listings from approximately the last 14 months; no future releases, no series, no rental-only Apple TV Store titles, no unrelated streaming providers. Up to ten unique movies, randomized deterministically per six-hour cycle (and on manual refresh where offered). The actual number may be below 10 if TMDB does not return enough verified candidates.
- **Movie artwork:** Only titles with a useful backdrop and minimum catalog popularity are selected; no fake artwork or fabricated movie availability. Films already in your Watchlist may still feature in the top ten.
- **Playback vs availability:** TMDB/JustWatch catalog listings indicate reported provider availability in the US, not a promise that the selected playback source carries any particular stream.
- **Third-party player limitation:** The embed's exposed `branding` and `color` URL parameters can be requested by ZeroPlay, but an externally hosted player's own embedded logo is controlled by that provider. Its internal image cannot be guaranteed to change.
