# ZeroPlay 2.2.6.6 — Four Additional Movie & TV Sources

## Added playback providers

New selectable providers for TMDB movie and series playback on Android, Android TV, Android Lite, Android TV Lite, Windows, and web:

- **MappleTV (4K HDR)** — documented playback endpoint: `https://mapple.fun/watch/movie/{tmdb_id}` and TV `/watch/tv/{tmdb_id}-{season}-{episode}`.
- **Vidly (4K)** — `https://vidly.pro/movie/{tmdb_id}` or `/tv/{tmdb_id}/{season}/{episode}`.
- **Vidcore (4K)** — `https://vidcore.org/embed/movie/{tmdb_id}` or `/embed/tv/{tmdb_id}/{season}/{episode}`.
- **Chillflix (4K)** — `https://www.chillflix.pw/embed/movie/{tmdb_id}` or TV equivalent, requesting `prefer4k=1`.

The providers' labels reflect their advertised 4K capability; actual resolution, availability, rights and reliability are determined by each source.

## Stability and compatibility

- Retains existing provider ordering and fallback behavior, with RawCast still last in Android/Windows.
- Uses exact HTTPS player-host trust allowlists and preserves Android TV and Windows redirect restrictions.
- Keeps Android-controlled next-episode navigation authoritative for supported embeds.
- Adds source URL and domain trust regression tests and improves Windows/web playback progress support for Mapple/Chillflix/Vidly events.
- Includes the new sources in device-local Smart Playback health scoring. A selected global source remains preferred unless measured playback reliability favors another option.

## Builds

Official signed Android and Android TV editions, mobile and TV Lite editions, and Windows x64 portable ZIP with ZeroPlayUpdater.exe.

## Source responsibility

ZeroPlay does not control third-party availability or licensing. Use embedded playback only when authorized by the content rightsholders and provider terms.
