# ZeroPlay 2.2.6.5 — Faster Home, TMDB Popular Carousel

## Android / Android TV
- Replace the streaming-specific Top 10 hero with **What's Popular on TMDB** from TMDB's official /movie/popular endpoint, refreshed at every application startup.
- Persist successful Popular carousel results in device storage. Display cached picks immediately while TMDB refreshes in the background and without waiting for the longer full home catalog aggregation.
- Remove the carousel's streaming-provider logo/name lookup and the Refresh Picks button. Keep the Streaming Provider Hub and other screens intact.

## Windows
- Display the TMDB Popular movies carousel without streaming-provider badge/logo or Refresh Picks.
- Remove up-to-eight subscription-provider lookups, and fetch independent home lists concurrently.
- Present the Popular hero as soon as its single request returns, then fill additional homepage shelves.

## Compatibility
- No playback, stream selection, updater, source, watch history, favorites or theme behavior changes.
- Official Android Mobile, Android TV, Mobile Lite and TV Lite signed APKs versionCode **70**, versionName **2.2.6.5**.
- Windows x64 ZIP includes separate ZeroPlayUpdater.exe; Electron internal version **2.2.6-5**.
- Release only after successful regression tests, signed Android artifacts, Windows packaging and smoke checks.
