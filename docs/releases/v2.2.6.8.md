# ZeroPlay 2.2.6.8 — MappleTV Comprehensive Ad Filtering

- Target known advertising and tracking networks in MappleTV with exact-domain and subdomain matching in Android and Windows native players.
- Remove dynamically inserted ad overlays, dialogs and ad iframe creatives in MappleTV and its player frames, including open shadow roots.
- Keep the top-right Watch options button hidden after three seconds of inactivity; reveal it again on user interaction.
- Preserve video, audio, controls, existing sources, and all signed build variants.
- Does not indiscriminately block unknown video hosts or automatically follow/click ad links.
- Third-party providers can change advertising mechanisms. No filter can guarantee every possible future first-party or encrypted ad is suppressed.
