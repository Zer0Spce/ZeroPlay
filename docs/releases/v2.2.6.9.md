# ZeroPlay v2.2.6.9 — Restore MappleTV 2.2.6.7 and VidStuck Default

- Restore the MappleTV player and cosmetic guard exactly to the v2.2.6.7 implementations.
- Remove the extra MappleTV ad-network interception introduced in v2.2.6.8.
- One-time migration resets global default to **VidStuck** and switches off Smart Playback selection and automatic source recovery on Android, Windows and web.
- Reset previously saved per-title Vidly choices, while preserving other selected sources and video-library data.
- MappleTV, Vidly, Vidcore and Chillflix remain available as manually selectable providers.
- New signed version leaves the previously published 2.2.6.7 release unchanged.

Note: MappleTV's own advertising may continue under its original integration.
